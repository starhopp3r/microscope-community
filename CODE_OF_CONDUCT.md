# Code of conduct

Microscope community is a place to share biology figures that explain things well. Keep it that way.

## Expected

- Be kind and specific in reviews and comments. Critique the figure, not the person.
- Share only work you made, or have the right to share under the license you pick.
- Credit ideas and references you built on, in the figure's README.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Figures or text that are hateful, sexual, violent, or meant to mislead.
- Copied work presented as your own, or work shared without the right to share it.
- Figures that try to track, attack or deceive the people who view them, or that try to get around the checks.
- Spam, advertising, or mass submissions.

## What happens

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close pull requests and issues, remove figures, and block people from the repository. Serious or repeated cases, and any attempt to attack viewers or the checks, lead to a permanent block.

## Reporting

- **A figure** that breaks these rules, infringes someone's rights or looks unsafe: use [Report a figure](../../issues/new?template=report-figure.yml).
- **A person's behaviour,** or anything you'd rather not post publicly: contact a maintainer privately through GitHub (@starhopp3r).
- **A security problem** in the checks: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Don't open a public issue for it.

Reports are handled as confidentially as possible.
